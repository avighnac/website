Colleges

A public documentation of the colleges I applied to, and the results of these applications.

# USA

I applied to the following colleges, for computer science where I had to choose or list a major:

  • MIT
  • Princeton
  • Stanford
  • UIUC
  • CMU
  • Brown
  • Caltech

I applied for financial aid everywhere except UIUC, which does not offer need-based financial aid to international students.

I got into UIUC, and this is where I'll be going. I was rejected from everywhere else.

# India

In India, I applied to:

  • IIIT Hyderabad: Selected for the interview, after which I was rejected.
  • IIT Gandhinagar: A straightforward online application. I received an offer for CSE.
  • IIT Kanpur: An offline JEE-style exam held at IIT Kanpur itself. I did atrociously badly here, but still got into Intelligent Systems.
  • IIT Kharagpur: I received an offer for CSE through SCOPE in the second round, under a new admissions pathway introduced for olympiad medalists.
  • IIT Mandi: I received an offer for CSE here as well, in the first SCOPE round.

# Singapore

Here, I only applied to NUS and NTU, both for computer science. I was unfortunately rejected from both. I suspect this may have been due in part to my relatively low board marks (92%), though I of course don't know the exact reason.

# UK

Did not apply.
